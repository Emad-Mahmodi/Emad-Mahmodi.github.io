A results-driven Senior Software Engineer and Reverse Engineering Researcher with 10+ years of experience in high-performance computing, automotive embedded systems, and AI-driven cybersecurity. Proven expertise in firmware analysis (TriCore, PowerPC), binary de-obfuscation, and the integration of LLMs with automated diagnostic tools. Published researcher in reputable journals with a focus on anomaly detection and system security.

My research interests span **AI in Cybersecurity** (leveraging LLMs and RAG for automated binary analysis), **Binary Reverse Engineering** of automotive ECUs, **Data Science & Anomaly Detection** for high-frequency data streams, and **Systems Security** including anti-debugging and hardware-level vulnerability research, and Recommendation system .

At [Negar-Khodro](https://negarkhodro.com), I architect ECU programming platforms, lead reverse engineering efforts for TriCore and PowerPC architectures, and develop tools like *Negaremap* — a native Persian remapping platform with automated table identification and checksum correction. I have also contributed to high-performance computing projects at Greenchall, porting AI inference models to edge devices, and conducted vulnerability research at NIUniverse & Rahgozin Rayaneh.

I hold an M.Sc. in Computer Science (Software Engineering) from **Ferdowsi University of Mashhad**, where my thesis focused on adaptive phishing detection against URL obfuscation. My work has been published in *Expert Systems with Applications* (Elsevier), *Electronics* (MDPI), and the *International Journal of Engineering*, with further papers currently under review at *Future Generation Computer Systems* (Elsevier).
